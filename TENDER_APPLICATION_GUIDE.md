# How to Apply for Indian Tyre Companies' Rubber Scrap Tenders

## 1) Companies to target first
Focus on large tyre manufacturers and major plants in India that periodically dispose of:
- curing bladders and rejected compounds
- bead/rubberized fabric waste
- off-spec tread/sidewall compounds
- buffing dust and reclaimable scrap

Create a tracking sheet with:
- Company name
- Plant location
- Scrap type
- Tender portal link
- Vendor registration status
- EMD/SD requirements
- Last bid date

## 2) Where tenders are published
Check these channels weekly (some daily):
- Company procurement pages ("Tenders", "Procurement", "E-auction")
- E-procurement platforms used by private manufacturers
- MSTC/e-auction listings (for scrap auctions)
- State pollution-board approved recycler/vendor lists (for compliance-linked scrap)

Tip: set alerts with keywords: `rubber scrap`, `tyre scrap`, `off-spec rubber`, `auction`, `disposal`.

## 3) Mandatory business setup before applying
Keep these ready in PDF and scanned signed copies:
- GST registration
- PAN and business constitution proof (proprietorship/partnership/company)
- UDYAM/MSME (if applicable)
- Factory license/trade license
- Consent/authorization for handling waste (if required by tender scope)
- Recent ITR and audited financials
- Bank solvency / net-worth certificate (if asked)
- Cancelled cheque and bank details
- Past performance / purchase orders for similar scrap

## 4) Vendor registration workflow
Most tyre companies require pre-qualification before allowing bidding:
1. Submit vendor onboarding form
2. Upload KYC, statutory documents, and contact details
3. Accept compliance clauses (EHS, labor, anti-bribery)
4. Complete site/yard capability declaration
5. Receive vendor code and bidding access

Keep one shared email and phone dedicated for tenders so no bid updates are missed.

## 5) Financial and bidding requirements
Typical tender terms include:
- EMD (Earnest Money Deposit)
- Security Deposit/Performance BG after award
- Lot-wise bidding and lifting timelines
- As-is-where-is material condition
- Taxes, loading, transport, weighment, and gate pass responsibilities

Before bidding, calculate landed cost:
`Bid price + GST + loading + transport + handling losses + financing cost`

Only bid where post-processing margin remains positive.

## 6) Compliance checkpoints (critical)
For rubber/industrial scrap, verify:
- Waste category and whether authorization is needed
- Storage/transport norms for the material class
- Manifest/challan documentation trail
- E-way bill and invoicing compliance
- Plant entry safety requirements (PPE, insurance, vehicle documents)

Non-compliance can cause blacklisting across plants.

## 7) Commercial strategy to win sustainably
- Start with smaller lots to build performance history
- Quote aggressively only where logistics are favorable
- Maintain strict lifting discipline to avoid penalties
- Share periodic utilization/recycling declarations if requested
- Build relationships with plant purchase + scrap yard teams professionally

Consistency often matters more than one-time highest bids.

## 8) Simple action plan for the next 30 days
Week 1:
- Build target list of tyre plants and portals
- Compile all statutory and financial documents

Week 2:
- Complete vendor registrations for top 10 targets
- Activate keyword alerts and auction notifications

Week 3:
- Participate in 3-5 small/medium scrap tenders
- Track bid-vs-winning price benchmark

Week 4:
- Review win/loss reasons
- Refine pricing model by location and scrap type
- Expand to additional plants/auction platforms

## 10) Bid/no-bid checklist
Bid only if all are YES:
- Vendor registration approved
- EMD capacity available
- Compliance documents valid
- Transport route confirmed
- Processing/reuse outlet confirmed
- Positive margin after all costs
- Team ready for timely lifting
